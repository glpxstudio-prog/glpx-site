Why shoot in Downtown Orlando?

Downtown is where a lot of Orlando's offices, law firms and startups live. The mix of glass towers, brick and greenery makes it easy to shoot polished and personal in the same hour.

Downtown has hotel ballrooms, rooftop venues and the Dr. Phillips Center for company events.

I usually work with attorneys, finance pros, startup founders and downtown office teams here.
