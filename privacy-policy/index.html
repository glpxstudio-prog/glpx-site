Privacy policy

Last updated January 15, 2025

This Privacy Notice for glpxstudio ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our photography services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at https://glpxstudio.com
  • Book photography sessions
  • Opt-in to receive SMS/text messages
  • Engage with us in other related ways

SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice:

What personal information do we process? We process personal information you provide when booking photography sessions, communicating with us, opting in to SMS messages, or visiting our website.

Do we process sensitive personal information? No, we do not process sensitive personal information.

Do we collect information from third parties? No, we do not collect information from third parties.

How do we process your information? We process your information to provide photography services, send communications including SMS messages, process payments, and comply with legal obligations.

In what situations do we share personal information? We may share information with service providers like payment processors and SMS platforms that help us deliver our services.

How do we keep your information safe? We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information.

What are your rights? Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Book photography services
  • Express interest in our services
  • Opt-in to receive text messages
  • Contact us via phone, email, or website
  • Participate in activities on our website

The personal information we collect includes:

  • Contact Information: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, mailing addresses
  • Business Information: Company name, job title, professional details
  • Billing Information: Billing addresses, payment details
  • Session Information: Photography preferences, location preferences, scheduling details
  • Communication Records: Emails, messages, consultation notes

SMS/Text Messaging Information

When you opt-in to receive text messages from GLPX Studio, we collect:

  • Mobile phone numbers
  • Carrier information
  • Opt-in/opt-out preferences and timestamps
  • Message delivery confirmations
  • Message interaction data (responses, clicks)
  • Communication preferences
  • Consent records and methods

This information is used solely for delivering service-related and promotional messages as described in our Terms and Conditions.

Payment Information

We may collect data necessary to process payments for our photography services, such as:

  • Payment instrument number
  • Security code
  • Cardholder name
  • Billing address

All payment data is handled and stored by our payment processor Stripe. You may find their privacy notice at: https://stripe.com/privacy

Information Automatically Collected

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited on our site
  • Time and date of visit

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

Service Delivery

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication
  • To deliver photography services you've requested
  • To manage bookings and scheduling
  • To deliver edited photographs via secure galleries

Communications

  • To send SMS/text messages to users who have explicitly opted in, including:
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Gallery delivery notifications
  • Promotional offers and seasonal specials
  • Session updates and rescheduling notices
  • Payment reminders
  • To send administrative emails about your services
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To request feedback about our services

Business Operations

  • To process payments and manage orders
  • To protect our services from fraud and abuse
  • To analyze usage trends and improve our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Marketing (With Consent)

  • To send promotional communications to opted-in users
  • To deliver targeted content based on your preferences
  • To inform you about new services or special offers

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION?

We may share your personal information in the following situations:

Service Providers

We share information with third-party vendors who perform services for us:

  • Payment Processors: Stripe for payment processing
  • SMS Platforms: For delivering text messages to opted-in users
  • Cloud Storage: For secure image storage and delivery
  • Email Services: For sending service-related emails
  • Analytics Providers: Google Analytics for website optimization

Business Transfers

We may share or transfer your information in connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of our business.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when legally required to do so, such as in response to a court order or government request.

With Your Consent

We may share your personal information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

4. SMS/TEXT MESSAGING PRIVACY

SMS Data Collection and Use

When you opt-in to receive text messages:

  • We collect and store your mobile number securely
  • We track opt-in/opt-out status and timestamps
  • We monitor message delivery for quality assurance
  • We never sell your phone number to third parties
  • We only use your number for GLPX Studio communications

Opting Out of SMS

You can opt-out of SMS messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message
  • Emailing info@glpxstudio.com
  • Calling (407) 534-7581

SMS Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your mobile number and SMS data. Access to SMS data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. DO WE USE COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

Yes, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain website security
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Improve user experience

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain your personal information for:

  • Active Clients: Throughout our business relationship and 6 months after last service
  • SMS Data: Until you opt-out, plus any legally required retention period
  • Financial Records: 7 years for tax and accounting purposes
  • Photography Files: 1 year minimum, unless otherwise agreed
  • Marketing Lists: Until you unsubscribe

When retention is no longer necessary, we securely delete or anonymize your information.

7. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures:

Technical Measures

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure password requirements
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive data
  • Firewall protection

Organizational Measures

  • Limited access to personal information
  • Employee confidentiality agreements
  • Regular security training
  • Vendor security assessments
  • Incident response procedures

Photography-Specific Security

  • Password-protected client galleries
  • Encrypted cloud storage for images
  • Regular backups of all photography files
  • Secure file transfer protocols

While we strive to protect your information, no electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. You transmit information at your own risk.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

Access and Portability

  • Request copies of your personal information
  • Receive your data in a portable format

Correction and Deletion

  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information

Opt-Out Rights

  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Opt-out of SMS messages
  • Opt-out of cookies and tracking

Additional Rights (Location-Specific)

  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to non-discrimination

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@glpxstudio.com or (407) 534-7581.

Most browsers include Do-Not-Track (DNT) features. We currently do not respond to DNT signals as no uniform standard has been adopted. We will update this policy if we begin responding to DNT signals in the future.

  • Internet Activity: Website browsing data
  • Professional Information: Business details for corporate clients

Your California Rights

  • Right to know what information we collect
  • Right to delete your information
  • Right to opt-out of "sales" (we don't sell your data)
  • Right to non-discrimination

To exercise these rights, California residents can contact us at info@glpxstudio.com.

11. VIRGINIA, COLORADO, CONNECTICUT, AND UTAH RESIDENTS

Residents of these states have similar privacy rights including:

We are based in the United States. If you access our services from outside the US, please be aware that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction.

Parents who believe we may have collected information from their child should contact us immediately at info@glpxstudio.com.
