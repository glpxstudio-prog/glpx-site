Before the call

Four things to think about.

None of these need perfect answers. They just help us get to a plan faster.

  1. Where will the photos be used? Website, LinkedIn, Instagram, an album, a press kit?
  2. Is there a deadline, like a launch, a listing or a release date?
  3. Is it just you, or a team or group?
  4. Studio, on location, or both?

Quick answers

Booking details.

How do I secure a date?

A 50% deposit secures your date. The balance is due the day of the session.

Where are sessions held?

Studio sessions take place in Orlando. On-location shoots can happen anywhere in the city, including Downtown, Winter Park, Lake Nona, Baldwin Park, Dr. Phillips and Windermere. Full Day sessions include travel within one hour.

¿Hablas español?

Sí. Las sesiones pueden ser en español o en inglés.

What does it cost?

Every shoot is quoted to fit what you need: how many people, looks, locations and images. Book a free strategy call and I'll send a quote the same day.
