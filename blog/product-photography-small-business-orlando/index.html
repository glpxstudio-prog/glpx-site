Product Photography for Small Businesses in Orlando: What to Know Before You Book

By Gerson Lopez · GLPX Studio, Orlando FL

Good product photography for a small business starts with a list of where every image will run, then pairs clean studio shots that show exactly what you sell with lifestyle shots that show how it feels to use it. Plan both in one session and you get a full set for your website, marketplace listings, ads and social media.

If you sell candles, skincare, sauces, clothing, jewelry or anything else people buy from a photo, your product images are doing the job a salesperson would do in a store. I'm Gerson Lopez of GLPX Studio. I shoot commercial and product work in Orlando for local brands, and here is what to know before you book.

What kinds of product photos does a small business need?

Most small brands need three types:

  • Clean product shots. The product alone on a plain background, usually white or a light neutral. These are your store listings, your catalog and your marketplace images.
  • Lifestyle shots. The product in use or in a real setting: a candle on a nightstand, a serum in someone's hand, a sauce on a plate. These sell the feeling and work best on social and in ads.
  • Detail shots. Close-ups of texture, labels, ingredients, stitching or packaging. They answer the questions a customer would ask if they could pick the product up.

Add people to the mix and you get the images that build trust fastest: your team making the product, packing orders or talking to customers.

Where will your product photos be used?

This is the most important question, because each platform needs a different shape:

Where it runs Shape to plan for
Online store and catalog Square, consistent background and lighting
Website banner Wide, with empty space for a headline
Instagram feed Square or 4:5 vertical
Stories, Reels and TikTok 9:16 vertical
Ads A set of square and vertical versions of the same idea

Marketplaces like Amazon have their own rules for the main listing image, including a pure white background, so check the requirements for every place you sell before the shoot. I plan the shot list around them so nothing gets cropped awkwardly later.

Studio or on location?

Clean product shots belong in a studio, where the light and background stay consistent from the first product to the fiftieth. I shoot in professional rented studios in Orlando.

Lifestyle shots can happen in a studio set or on location: your shop, your kitchen, a client's home or somewhere in Orlando that fits your brand. Many small businesses do both in one day, studio first, then a short on-location set.

How do you prepare your products for a photoshoot?

  • Bring your best units. Pick the cleanest, unscratched, perfectly labeled pieces, and bring backups.
  • Clean everything. Wipe off fingerprints and dust, and remove price stickers.
  • Steam fabric. Clothing, bags and linens should be pressed and on hangers.
  • Bring all variations. Every color, size and scent you want shown, clearly labeled.
  • Bring props that fit your brand. Linens, plates, plants or ingredients that match your colors.
  • Send a product list. Names, quantities and which shots each product needs.

Food and beauty products need extra care. Bring enough to replace anything that melts, wilts or smudges under the lights.

Should you add models or video?

If people use your product on their body, like clothing, jewelry or skincare, a model shows scale and makes the product feel real. If you plan to run ads, short vertical video of the product in use usually earns more attention than a still photo. Both can be added to the same production, along with hair and makeup and creative direction.

How is product photography priced?

Every commercial shoot is different, so I quote each one after a free call. What changes the quote: the number of products, how many shots each product needs, studio or location, models, styling and video. Most product shoots fit into one of two productions:

  • Half day: up to 5 hours with up to 3 set changes, enough for a focused product line with a mix of clean and lifestyle shots.
  • Full day: up to 10 hours with unlimited locations and travel within 1 hour of Orlando, for a full catalog or a campaign.

Edits are added per image, so you only pay for the finals you need. A 50% deposit secures your date.

The practical takeaway

Before you book, make two lists: every product you want shot, and every place the photos will run. Those two lists tell your photographer how long the shoot needs to be, what shapes to capture and how many edits you need.

Questions

How many products can you shoot in one day?

It depends on how many angles and setups each product needs. A simple white-background shot goes fast. A styled lifestyle scene takes longer. Send your product list and I will tell you what fits.

Can you shoot products I ship to you?

Ask on our call. Most local brands bring their products to the studio on shoot day, which also lets you approve the look as we go.

Do you photograph food and drinks?

Yes, for restaurants, caterers and food brands. Plan for extra product, since food needs to look fresh under the lights.

Can I use the photos for ads?

Your standard license covers branding, social media, websites, print and portfolios. Paid advertising, resale and third-party licensing need a separate commercial license, which I quote with the shoot.

Do you offer sessions in Spanish?

Yes. I work in English and Spanish with Orlando businesses.
