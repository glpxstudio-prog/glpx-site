How Much Does Branding Photography Cost in Orlando? What Goes Into Your Quote

By Gerson Lopez · GLPX Studio, Orlando FL

The cost of branding photography in Orlando depends on four things: how long we shoot, how many edited images you need, where we shoot, and what you add on, like hair and makeup or video. A one-hour headshot and a full content day are very different jobs, so instead of a price list, here is exactly what shapes a branding quote and what you get for it.

I'm Gerson Lopez, founder of GLPX Studio. I have 8+ years behind the camera, from album covers for Alex Rose and Anuel AA to branding sessions for Orlando founders, realtors and small business owners. Every shoot I do is quoted after a free call, and these are the questions that call answers.

What is branding photography, and how is it different from a headshot?

Branding photography is a planned set of images that shows who you are, what you do and how it feels to work with you. A headshot is one part of that. A branding session adds your workspace, your product, your process and the details that make your business recognizable.

Think of a headshot as your business card and a branding session as your whole visual toolkit: website banners, an About page portrait, Instagram posts, LinkedIn, press kits and ads. If you are not sure which one you need, read branding photos vs headshots.

What changes the price of a branding shoot?

Five things move a branding quote up or down.

  1. Time on set. Two hours gets you a focused set of looks. A half day lets us change outfits, setups and locations without rushing.
  2. Number of edited images. Each delivered image is selected, color graded and retouched. More finals means more editing time.
  3. Location. I shoot in professional rented studios in Orlando and on location, from a Downtown Orlando office to a Winter Park storefront. Studio rental is its own line so you can see exactly what it costs.
  4. Creative direction. Moodboards, shot lists and styling before the shoot are where a lot of the value lives. It is also why the photos look intentional instead of random.
  5. Add-ons. Hair and makeup, behind-the-scenes stills and short Reels from the same day all add to the quote, and all save you from booking a second production.

Is a cheap branding photographer worth it?

A lower price is worth it only if the images still do their job. The hidden cost of a cheap shoot is reshooting six months later because the photos do not match your brand, or because you only got three usable frames.

Before you compare prices, compare what is included: planning, the number of edited images, the retouching level and usage rights. Two quotes for the same amount can deliver very different results.

How many photos do you get from a branding session?

My branding packages deliver 10 to 15 retouched images, chosen from the strongest frames of the day. Half-day and full-day sessions price edits per image, so you only pay for the photos you actually want.

Ask any photographer how many retouched finals are included, not just how many photos they take. We will shoot hundreds of frames on the day. What matters is how many polished images you receive.

How does the process work?

Every project follows the same four steps:

  1. Free strategy call. Goals, budget, timeline, and where the photos will live. You get a custom quote within 24 hours.
  2. Creative direction. Moodboard, shot list, wardrobe and location plan.
  3. Shoot day. A professional rented studio in Orlando or a location that fits your brand. I direct every pose, so you never have to guess what to do with your hands.
  4. Delivery. Edited, print-ready images, sized for web and print.

A 50% deposit secures your date. Sessions run in English or Spanish.

How should you budget for branding photos?

Budget for the photos you will use over the next 6 to 12 months, not just the next post. If you plan to refresh your website, launch a service and stay consistent on Instagram, one longer session usually costs less per image than three separate small shoots.

If budget is tight, start with a strong headshot and a handful of branding images for your website. You can always add a content session later with the same look.

The practical takeaway

Before you ask for quotes, write down three things: where the photos will be used, how many images you need, and your launch date. Those three answers turn a vague price range into an accurate quote.
