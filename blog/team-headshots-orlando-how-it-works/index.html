Corporate Team Headshots in Orlando: A 2026 Guide

By Gerson Lopez · GLPX Studio, Orlando FL

Corporate team headshots work when every photo looks like it came from the same company: same background, same light, same crop and a clear wardrobe guide. At GLPX Studio in Orlando, every team session starts with a free strategy call. Then we set up a portable studio in your office or book a professional studio, and photograph each person in a few minutes so the workday keeps moving.

That is the short answer. Here is the full plan.

Why does a company need consistent team headshots?

A client lands on your "Meet the Team" page and sees a car selfie, a cropped wedding photo and a gray-backdrop studio portrait. What they read is disorganization, no matter how good your service is.

A consistent headshot set tells people the company is organized, the team is real and someone is paying attention to details. That matters for Orlando service businesses: law offices, clinics, insurance agencies, real estate brokerages, accounting firms and tech companies competing for talent.

In the office or in a studio?

Both work. The right choice depends on team size and how long people can step away from their desks.

  • In your office: best for teams of about 8 or more. We bring the backdrop, lights and everything else. Each person walks in, gets photographed and goes back to work. Nobody fights I-4 traffic.
  • At a professional studio in Orlando: best for small teams or a more editorial look. Studio rental is billed separately.
  • Outside Orlando: a travel fee applies beyond 30 miles from Orlando, for example in Clermont or Daytona.

Not sure which fits? Read studio or on location.

How long does each person take?

Once the set is up, each corporate headshot takes just a few minutes per person, with time to coach pose and expression. A large team fits in one morning or a half-day session, which includes up to 5 hours on set. The biggest time saver is a block schedule: everyone knows their time slot and shows up ready.

How to plan the session, step by step

  1. Free strategy call. We decide the style (white, gray, brand-color backdrop or a softly blurred office), the crop and where the photos will live: website, LinkedIn, badges, proposals or press.
  2. Team list. You send names and titles. We build the block schedule and name the files so they are easy to upload.
  3. Wardrobe guide. We send a short guide in English and Spanish so everyone gets it in their language.
  4. Session day. We set up, test the light and start. I coach every pose and expression, so nobody needs to "know how to pose."
  5. Selection and delivery. Each person picks a favorite in an online gallery. You get edited files sized for web and print.

What should employees wear?

The simple rule: solid colors, no big logos and no tiny patterns that shimmer on camera. If your company has brand colors, a touch of one (a blouse, a tie, a blazer) ties the photos to your visual identity.

Pick one formality level for everyone. If half the team wears suits and half wears polos, the set stops looking consistent. For the details, see what to wear for headshots.

What about new hires?

This is the problem almost nobody plans for. You hire someone in March and their photo does not match. The fix is to save the session "recipe": backdrop, light setup, distance and crop. GLPX Studio documents that setup so follow-up sessions match the original set.

How much do corporate headshots cost in Orlando?

It depends on headcount, office versus studio, and how many edited images each person needs. Every team gets a custom quote after the strategy call, and a 50% deposit secures the date. Studio rental, when needed, is billed separately. For a breakdown of what drives the price, see how much headshots cost in Orlando.

FAQs

Can you photograph our team at our Orlando office?

Yes. We bring the backdrop and lights and set up in a conference room or any clear open space, confirmed during the strategy call. A travel fee applies if the office is more than 30 miles from Orlando.

Can each person choose their own photo?

Yes. Everyone reviews their options in an online gallery and picks a favorite. Extra edited images can be added per image.

How long until we get the photos?

Edited images are delivered in about 2 to 3 weeks, sized for web and print.

What if someone misses session day?

We can photograph them later with the same light and backdrop setup so their photo matches the rest of the team.

Do you run sessions in Spanish?

Yes. Communication, the wardrobe guide and on-set coaching can be in English, Spanish or both. Leer en español.
