The Essential Role of Digitals in Your Modeling Career: Why You Need Them

By Gerson Lopez · GLPX Studio, Orlando FL

Why Every Model Needs a Digitals Photoshoot

In the competitive world of modeling, your digitals—also known as polaroids or snaps—are your professional calling card. They showcase your natural look and provide agencies and clients with an unfiltered, realistic view of your appearance. At GLPX Studio, we understand the significance of these simple yet powerful shots. Here's why a digitals photoshoot is essential for every model, whether you're a beginner or an established professional.

1. Show Your True Self

Digitals capture you as you are—no makeup, no retouching, and no elaborate outfits. They emphasize your natural features, proportions, and raw beauty, allowing agencies to envision you in various roles, from high fashion editorials to commercial campaigns. The more authentic your digitals, the better agencies can gauge your versatility and potential.

2. A Modeling Industry Standard

Most modeling agencies and casting directors require digitals as part of your application. These photos are a crucial element in determining whether a model fits a specific look or project. Even top models regularly update their digitals because clients always want the most recent and accurate representation of a model's appearance.

3. Highlight Your Range

A digitals photoshoot isn't just about showing your face. Full-body shots, close-ups, and profile angles are essential. These images highlight your body type, height, and posture, allowing agencies to see the full package. By including a range of angles and poses, you showcase your adaptability, making it easier for agencies and clients to visualize you in various contexts.

4. A Key to Growth and Development

For new models, digitals serve as an important tool for tracking progress. As you develop your skills and gain experience, you'll want to update your digitals to reflect your growth. This gives you and your agency a clear picture of how you're evolving, what works, and what areas might need improvement.

5. The Foundation for Your Modeling Portfolio

Before diving into more elaborate photoshoots, digitals are the perfect starting point for building your portfolio. They create a professional base that you can build upon as you gain more experience and start working with different photographers, stylists, and clients.

6. Quick Updates for Agencies and Clients

Digitals are an easy way to update your look without the hassle of full editorial shoots. If you change your hairstyle, build muscle, or alter your appearance, fresh digitals keep your portfolio current. This quick and efficient method ensures that your agency always has your most up-to-date images to send out for castings.
