Music Release Content Plan for Orlando Artists

By Gerson Lopez · GLPX Studio, Orlando FL

A music release needs more than cover art. The minimum set: square cover art for streaming, press photos, a vertical Spotify Canvas, vertical clips for Reels and TikTok, and behind-the-scenes photos to keep the conversation going. The best approach is to create all of it in one planned production with one visual concept, so the release feels like an era instead of scattered posts.

At GLPX Studio in Orlando, I have shot album covers for Alex Rose ("El Nuevo Rockstar" and "Oversized") and Anuel AA ("Las Leyendas Nunca Mueren"). Here is what I have learned about building a release.

What content does a release need?

Piece What it is for Format
Cover art Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, every platform Square 1:1
Press photos Blogs, media, promoters, EPK Horizontal and vertical, high resolution
Spotify Canvas The full-screen loop that plays in Spotify Vertical 9:16, 3 to 8 seconds
Reels and TikToks Teasers, countdown, release day Vertical 9:16
Behind-the-scenes photos Stories and posts for weeks Vertical and square
Profile and banner images Artist profiles on platforms and social Various sizes

What specs does Spotify require?

Cover art: square (1:1), JPG, PNG or TIFF, between 640 and 10,000 pixels per side, in sRGB color. Spotify asks for the highest resolution available and does not accept upscaled images. That is why the photo has to come out of camera with enough resolution.

Canvas: a vertical (9:16) loop of 3 to 8 seconds, MP4 or JPG, 720 to 1,080 pixels tall. It works best without anyone singing to camera, since the clip will not sync with the lyrics.

How far ahead should you plan?

A practical rule: start planning content about 6 to 8 weeks before release day.

  1. Weeks 8 to 6: concept. Strategy call, references, color palette, wardrobe and location. This is where the era's look gets defined.
  2. Weeks 6 to 4: production. One shoot day for cover, press photos, Canvas, vertical clips and BTS.
  3. Weeks 4 to 2: editing and delivery. Cover retouching, press selects and clip edits.
  4. Weeks 2 to 0: calendar. Teasers, cover reveal, countdown and release day.
  5. After release: BTS content keeps feeding your socials for weeks.

Why do it all in one production?

Because consistency sells the era. When the cover, Reels and press photos share light, color and wardrobe, fans recognize the project instantly. You also save: one studio day, one glam team, one creative direction.

GLPX Studio can cover photo and video on the same day, in half-day or full-day productions, with behind-the-scenes stills and Reels as add-ons. Studio rental is billed separately.

Who owns the cover?

Talk about usage rights before the shoot. Photos include a license for branding, social, web and print once paid in full. If the cover will go on merch or large paid ad campaigns, that needs a separate commercial license. Put it in writing up front and avoid problems later.

Mistakes that make a release look improvised

  • Pulling the cover from a low-resolution video frame.
  • Press photos with a different look than the cover.
  • Reaching release day with no vertical content.
  • Burning all the content in week one.
  • Designing the cover without checking how it reads as a phone thumbnail.

Takeaway: before you book a studio, write one sentence about what this era is. That sentence guides the palette, wardrobe, location and every piece of content.

FAQs

What content do I need to release a song?

At minimum: square cover art, 3 to 5 press photos, a Spotify Canvas, 3 to 5 vertical clips and BTS photos for Stories.

What size should Spotify cover art be?

Square, JPG, PNG or TIFF, between 640 and 10,000 pixels per side, in sRGB. Use the highest resolution you have and never upscale.

Do you shoot photo and video on the same day?

Yes. I work in both photo and video, so the cover, Canvas and Reels come from the same production.

Do you work with independent artists?

Yes. GLPX Studio works with independent artists, managers and labels in Orlando and Central Florida.
