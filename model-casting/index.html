Before you apply

Quick answers.

How often are the test shoots?

Once a month. Your application stays on file, so you can be picked for any month's concept without applying again.

Is it paid?

Test shoots are a trade (TFP). No payment either way, and you receive edited images to use in your portfolio.

Do I need experience?

No. New faces, working models and agency-signed talent are all welcome. Tell us where you are on the form.

Is hair and makeup provided?

It depends on the shoot. You'll know before you confirm, along with the look and the location.

Where are the shoots?

In an Orlando studio or on location around the city, depending on the concept.

¿Puedo aplicar en español?

Sí. Todo el proceso puede ser en español. Elige tu idioma en el formulario.

How old do I have to be?

18 or older. No exceptions.
