GLPX Studio · Client guide05

Shoot day

What to expect.

  • Arrive 10 minutes earlySettle in
  • Outfits on hangers, not foldedNo wrinkles
  • Water and a light snackStay fresh
  • Lip balm, comb, any touch upsSmall kit
  • Your shot list or inspirationScreenshots ok

The first few minutes are just warming up. Nobody looks their best in the first frames, and that's expected.

I'll tell you exactly where to stand, how to angle your shoulders and what to do with your hands. You'll see images on the back of the camera as we go, so you can relax knowing it's working.

Sessions run in English or Spanish, whichever feels more natural to you.
