About GLPX Studio

Hi, I'm Gerson.

Photographer and owner of GLPX Studio. I make branding, headshot and editorial photos for people who need to be remembered.

The short version

Eight years of putting people at ease in front of a lens.

I've been photographing people for more than eight years. That includes album covers for artists like Alex Rose and Anuel AA, as well as headshots for realtors squeezing a session into their lunch break.

Most people tell me they hate having their picture taken. That's the part I enjoy. I direct the whole session, from where to stand to what to do with your hands, so you can stop thinking about the camera and just show up as yourself.

I'm based in Thornton Park and shoot studio sessions in Orlando and on location across the city. Sessions run in English or Spanish.
