Do you shoot in Baldwin Park?

Yes. Baldwin Park is part of my regular area. On location sessions happen right in Baldwin Park, and studio sessions take place in Orlando.

Yes. Actor headshots get clean light and natural retouching so you look like yourself in the audition room. We can shoot a commercial and a theatrical look in one session.
