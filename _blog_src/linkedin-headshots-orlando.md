# LinkedIn Headshots in Orlando: How to Get One That Works
date: Nov 25, 2026

A strong LinkedIn headshot shows your face clearly, with soft even light, a background that does not distract and an expression that feels approachable and confident. Your face should fill most of the frame because LinkedIn shows it very small. The recommended size is 400 x 400 pixels in JPG or PNG, and it should look like the person who shows up to the meeting.

Now for the details that make the difference.

## Why does your LinkedIn photo matter so much?

Before anyone reads your experience, they see your photo: in search results, next to every comment and on every connection request. It is the first thing that connects your name to a real person. A dark photo, a crop from a group shot or a photo from ten years ago makes people hesitate before they message you.

For Orlando professionals working in bilingual markets, LinkedIn is often the front door to jobs, contracts and partnerships. A professional photo is one of the fastest upgrades you can make to your profile.

## What size and format does the photo need?

- **Recommended size:** 400 x 400 pixels (square, 1:1). Upload a larger, sharp version so it holds up on high-resolution screens.
- **Format:** JPG or PNG.
- **Max file size:** 8 MB.
- **Crop:** shoulders up, face centered. LinkedIn crops the photo into a circle, so leave a little room around your head.

## What makes a LinkedIn headshot work?

**Light.** Soft, frontal light removes harsh shadows under the eyes and keeps skin looking natural. An overcast window works. Orlando's midday sun does not.

**Expression.** You want approachable and confident at the same time. A slight smile that reaches the eyes works for most industries. On-set coaching is what gets you there: almost nobody looks natural when told to "smile."

**Background.** Gray, white, a solid color or a softly blurred office. It just cannot compete with your face.

**Wardrobe.** Dress the way you would for an important meeting in your industry. Solid colors, no logos. More ideas in [what to wear for headshots](blog/what-to-wear-for-headshots-orlando/).

**Freshness.** New haircut, new beard, or more than two or three years old? Time to update.

## The 7 most common mistakes

1. A photo cropped from a wedding or group shot.
2. Sunglasses or a hat.
3. A selfie with your arm in frame or a mirror shot.
4. A face that is too small inside the circle.
5. Heavy filters that change your skin tone.
6. Overhead light that puts shadows under your eyes.
7. A photo that no longer looks like you.

## Is a phone photo good enough?

It can work if you have no other option: face a window, use portrait mode, have someone shoot at eye level and take a lot of frames. But a phone does not coach you. What actually changes the result is someone who shapes the light to your face and guides your expression until you look like yourself on your best day.

If LinkedIn plays a real role in how you land work or clients, a professional headshot pays for itself.

## FAQs

### What size should a LinkedIn profile photo be?

LinkedIn recommends 400 x 400 pixels, square, in JPG or PNG, up to 8 MB. Upload a higher-resolution version so it stays sharp on every screen.

### What background color is best for LinkedIn?

A neutral backdrop like gray or white, or a solid color that fits your brand. A softly blurred office also works, as long as your face is the first thing people see.

### Should you smile in a LinkedIn photo?

In most industries, yes. A slight smile makes you look approachable. During the session we shoot options with and without a smile so you can choose.

### Can I use the same photo on LinkedIn and my website?

Yes. Using the same photo on LinkedIn, your website and your business profile helps people recognize you everywhere.
