# Actor Headshots in Orlando: Commercial vs Theatrical Looks
date: Oct 06, 2026

Orlando has a busy acting scene, from theme park entertainment and commercials to theater and film. Whatever you audition for, your headshot has one job: look exactly like the person who walks into the room. Here's how to get headshots casting directors can actually use.

## Commercial vs theatrical

Most actors need two looks.

**Commercial** is warm and approachable. Think a friendly smile, brighter colors and an expression that says "I'm the person you'd trust in this ad." It's used for commercials, print work and many theme park and brand roles.

**Theatrical** is moodier and more dramatic. Less smile, more intensity, often darker clothing and a little more contrast. It's used for film, TV and stage roles where casting wants to see range.

We can shoot both in one session by changing your wardrobe, the backdrop and the energy.

## What casting directors look for

- **It looks like you today.** Same hair, same weight, same style you'd walk in with.
- **Your eyes are sharp.** Focus and connection with the lens matter more than anything else.
- **Natural retouching.** Remove temporary things like a blemish, but keep your real skin, freckles and features.
- **Simple framing.** Your face should be the clear subject, with nothing distracting in the background.

## What to wear

- Solid colors in jewel tones or neutrals
- Simple necklines that frame your face
- No logos, no busy patterns, minimal jewelry
- Something that hints at your type without becoming a costume

Bring several tops so we can switch between your commercial and theatrical looks. For more ideas, see [what to wear for your headshots](blog/what-to-wear-for-headshots-orlando/index.html).
