# Studio or On Location? Where to Shoot Your Headshots in Orlando
date: Oct 06, 2026

When people search for a "photo studio near me," what they usually want is a clean, professional headshot without the hassle. In Orlando you have two good options: a studio session or an on location shoot at your office or somewhere around town. Here is how to choose.

## Studio sessions

For studio sessions I book a professional photo studio in Orlando. You get full control of the light, a seamless backdrop and zero distractions.

**Best for:**

- LinkedIn and corporate headshots
- Actor headshots for casting sites
- Anyone who wants a classic grey, white or charcoal background
- Bad weather days, which in Orlando means most summer afternoons

**What to expect:** a calm setup, guided posing and time to try different backdrops and outfits. You can see the results on the [headshots page](headshots.html).

## On location at your office

Instead of sending your team across town, I bring the lights and backdrop to you. It sets up in a conference room or a quiet corner, and each person only steps away from work for a few minutes.

**Best for:**

- Teams and offices that need matching headshots
- Medical practices, law firms and real estate teams
- Companies onboarding new hires through the year

The setup is the same as a studio, so every photo matches. More on [team and corporate headshots](corporate-headshots-orlando.html).

## On location around Orlando

Sometimes a backdrop is too formal. Shooting outside or in your own space gives you a headshot with context: city lights downtown, the oaks in Thornton Park, or brick and storefronts on Park Avenue in Winter Park.

**Best for:**

- Realtors, coaches and consultants who want a warmer look
- Personal brands that need a headshot plus lifestyle images
- Founders who want their business in the background

Shade is your friend in Florida. Early morning and the hour before sunset give the softest light. For location ideas, read [the best places for branding photos in Orlando](blog/best-places-for-branding-photos-in-orlando/index.html).

## Quick way to decide

- **Need a consistent look for a team?** On location at your office.
- **Need a clean, classic headshot?** Studio.
- **Need a headshot plus photos for social and your website?** A location shoot or a [branding session](branding.html).
