Why shoot in Thornton Park?

Thornton Park is where I'm based. Brick streets, an oak canopy and sidewalk cafes a few steps from Lake Eola make it one of my favorite places to shoot.

Thornton Park and the nearby downtown area have restaurants and lofts for smaller company events.

I usually work with creatives, small business owners, coaches and downtown professionals here.
